# Portfolio Copy Lab — implemented copy decisions

This is the copy decision sheet for the independent portfolio. The live preview uses the strongest concise version that fits each existing text slot. The framework is a ladder, not a requirement to make every sentence longer:

- **Good:** clearer skill, action or benefit.
- **Great:** connects that benefit to a desirable outcome.
- **Insane:** adds a substantiated metric, timeline or removes a real friction point.
- **Selection rule:** use the strongest rung that fits the original space naturally. Shorter wins when the longer line adds no meaningful persuasion.

## Case-study and experience copy

## Claim safeguards

- The **400K+** metric remains explicitly reported organic reach for associated short-video hooks; it is not attributed to a single line as a directly measured result.
- The Jay Academy film duration remains **3 minutes 44 seconds** and its CBFC Universal certificate date remains **16 June 2022**.
- The JK Tyre advertisement placement remains the front page of *Divya Marathi*, **23 June 2023**.
- No conversion rates, revenue increases, guaranteed results or turnaround promises have been added.
- Existing portfolio samples in Marathi, Hindi and English are retained as original client copy rather than rewritten as portfolio headlines.

## Test plan

Use the implemented concise copy as the baseline. Test one stronger proof-led line at a time. Judge results by relevant enquiries and brief quality, not clicks alone.
