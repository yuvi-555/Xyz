## Proof and claim guardrails

- The **400K+** figure is presented only as reported organic reach for associated short-video hooks. It is not described as independently audited or as a guaranteed result.
- The Jay Academy film duration is **3 minutes 44 seconds**; the CBFC Universal certificate date is **16 June 2022**.
- The JK Tyre advertisement appeared on the front page of *Divya Marathi* on **23 June 2023**.
- No conversion-rate, revenue-lift, turnaround-time or guaranteed-performance claims have been invented.

## Testing note

Start with the Great version as the control. Test one Insane headline or proof-led block at a time, keeping layout and traffic conditions as consistent as possible. Compare meaningful enquiries and brief quality, not clicks alone.
